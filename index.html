Candy Corn Jello Shots

Creamy white, orange, and lemon-yellow layers spiked with vodka, finished with a swirl of whipped cream and a candy corn on top.

Supplies

Vodka for this batch: 1½ cups (12 oz). A 375 ml bottle covers it.

Make them

Mix each new layer while the one before it chills, then pour once it's cooled to room temperature. Flip the switch on each step as you go.

  1. Set up the cups

    Line up 30 shot cups on a rimmed baking sheet so you can slide the whole tray in and out of the fridge.

  2. Bloom the gelatin

    Sprinkle 1 envelope of unflavored gelatin over ¼ cup cold water in a heatproof bowl. Don't stir. Let it sit until it looks wrinkly and fully soaked.

    5:00

  3. Make and pour the white layer

    Pour ¼ cup boiling water over the gelatin and stir until no grains remain. Whisk in ½ cup sweetened condensed milk, then stir in ½ cup vodka. Pour a scant tablespoon into each cup.

  4. Chill until tacky

    Refrigerate 30 to 45 minutes, until set but still a little sticky to the touch. That tackiness is what lets the next layer grab on.

    40:00

  5. Mix the orange layer

    While the white layer chills, stir 1 box of orange gelatin into 1 cup boiling water for 2 minutes, until fully dissolved. Stir in ½ cup cold water and ½ cup vodka. Let it cool on the counter to room temperature. Warm gelatin will melt the layer below it.

  6. Pour the orange layer and chill

    Pour about 1 tablespoon into each cup, slowly and over the back of a spoon so it doesn't dig into the white layer. Refrigerate 30 to 45 minutes, until tacky.

    40:00

  7. Mix the yellow layer

    While the orange layer chills, make the lemon layer the same way: stir 1 box of lemon gelatin into 1 cup boiling water, then add ½ cup cold water and ½ cup vodka. Cool it on the counter, not in the fridge, or it will start to set.

  8. Pour the yellow layer and set

    Pour about 1 tablespoon into each cup. Refrigerate at least 2 hours, or overnight, until completely firm. Snap the lids on if you're making them ahead.

    2:00:00

  9. Top and serve

    Right before serving, shake the whipped cream can well, hold it straight upside down, and spray a small swirl onto each shot. Press a candy corn into each swirl and serve cold. Canned whipped cream starts to deflate after about 15 minutes, so top the shots in rounds as guests arrive.

Tips for a good batch

Small things that make the layers neat and the party easier.

Make them ahead

With lids on, the layered shots keep 2 to 3 days in the fridge. Add the whipped cream and candy corn only when you serve.

Get clean, straight lines

Every layer should go in at room temperature, onto a layer that's set but still tacky. If the layer below is fully firm, the layers can separate. If it's still soft, the colors bleed together.

Know how strong they are

Each shot has about 0.4 oz of vodka, roughly a quarter of a standard shot. For stronger ones, use ¾ cup vodka and ¼ cup cold water per batch in the orange and yellow layers. More alcohol than that and they won't set well.

Add a little candy corn flavor

Candy corn tastes like honey and vanilla. Stirring ¼ teaspoon of vanilla extract into the white layer along with the vodka brings it closer.

Make some alcohol-free

Replace all of the vodka with cold water. Use a different cup color or a label so they don't get mixed in with the spiked ones.

Help them slide out

Tell guests to squeeze the cup or run a finger around the edge first. The whole shot loosens and slides out in one go.
